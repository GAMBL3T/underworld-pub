The game loop

Start small. Take over the city.

Members can understand the basics in a minute, while long-term progression gives them a reason to come back.

  1. 01

    Make dirty cash

    Claim your daily cut, run a hustle, or take your chances in a heist.

    /daily /hustle /heist
  2. 02

    Clean it up

    Launder dirty cash into clean money. Higher Heat means a higher fee.

    /launder
  3. 03

    Build your operation

    Spend clean money on rackets that earn dirty cash while you are away.

    /buy_racket /collect
  4. 04

    Protect your progress

    Watch your Heat, manage collection risk, and climb your server’s leaderboard.

    /heat /leaderboard

Built for communities

More than another balance command.

Underworld is designed to create friendly competition and daily activity without turning your server into a casino.

01

A real progression loop

Dirty and clean cash serve different purposes. Members earn, convert, invest, and grow instead of watching one number go up.

02

Passive racket income

Five rackets create a clear path from a street corner to a full operation, with capped offline earnings that reward returning.

03

Heat changes the risk

Activity attracts attention. Heat affects laundering fees and collection risk, then decays while players cool off.

04

Daily reasons to return

Streaks, return bonuses, server leaderboards, and profile milestones keep progression moving without constant grinding.

05

Tools for server staff

Manage Server controls let staff disable the economy, configure audit notices, and see Founding Server progress.

06

Privacy controls included

Members can remove their stored game data with /forgetme. Underworld does not sell data or store message content.

Questions

Before you add it.

Is Underworld free?

Yes. There are no purchases, subscriptions, paid boosts, or pay-to-win benefits.

Does progress carry between servers?

No. Every Discord server has its own independent economy, balances, leaderboards, and progression.

Is any real money involved?

No. All currency and rewards are fictional. They cannot be bought, sold, traded, transferred, or exchanged for anything of real-world value.

What permissions does the bot need?

The installer needs Manage Server. Underworld requests the Discord permissions needed to view and send messages, embed links, read message history, and register application commands.

Can members delete their data?

Yes. Members can run /forgetme and choose whether to remove their data from one server or every server.
